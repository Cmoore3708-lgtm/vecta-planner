VECTA Workshop Pro V41.26 — SMALL DEPLOYMENT BUILD

Upload the CONTENTS of this folder to the root of the existing GitHub repository.
No npm install or manual build is required. index.html is the live application.

After Vercel deploys, press Ctrl+F5 once.

Changes in this build:
- The Due next 30 days list now shows one main entry per vehicle.
- The vehicle's earliest due item remains on the main row.
- Any additional work due within 30 days appears immediately underneath as a smaller badge-and-date row.
- The Due next 30 days count now reflects vehicles rather than duplicated work rows.
- Selecting Staff on a job card opens a required Name field.
- A new CONTRACTOR option is available in the Customer / Income Stream menu.
- Selecting CONTRACTOR opens a required Name field for the new contractor company.
- Generic contractor jobs are grouped under the entered contractor name for end-of-month invoicing.
- Generic contractor invoice emails can use the email saved on the job card.
- All V41.25 fleet colour, badge, sorting and invoice behaviour remains in place.
