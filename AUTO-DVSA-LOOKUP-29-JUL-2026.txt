AUTOMATIC DVSA VEHICLE LOOKUP

- Removed the manual "Lookup DVSA MOT" button from the job card.
- Vehicle lookup now starts automatically after a valid-looking registration has been entered and typing has paused.
- A faster lookup also runs when the registration field is changed or left.
- Automatically fills the vehicle description and latest MOT mileage where available.
- Automatically stores make, model, fuel type, colour, engine size, MOT expiry/status, MOT history and current advisories on the job.
- Current MOT advisories and MOT expiry continue to populate Major, Interim and 6-month paperwork.
- Repeated requests for the same registration are suppressed.
- Stale responses are ignored if the registration is changed while a lookup is running.
- A failed DVSA lookup does not block manual job-card completion.
