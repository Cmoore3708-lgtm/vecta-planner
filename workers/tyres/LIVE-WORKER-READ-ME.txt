VECTA TYRE RECOVERY UPDATE — 31 October 2026

WHEN YOU ARE BACK AT THE WORKSHOP PC
1. Extract All from the ZIP.
2. Run INSTALL-UPDATE.cmd on your existing paired PC.
3. Use the new VECTA Tyre Worker desktop shortcut.
4. If ESP asks, sign in and check Vecta / Sellout / Include VAT / multiplier 1.
   The worker now resumes automatically. You do not need to press Enter.
5. Optional: run SAVE-SUPPLIER-LOGIN.cmd once to save your existing ESP login.
   The password is entered hidden and encrypted for your Windows account on this PC.
   It is not stored in this ZIP or sent to Vecta servers. Restart the tyre worker
   after saving it. Delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\VectaTyres\supplier-login.xml to remove it.

WHAT RECOVERS AUTOMATICALLY
- Temporary internet/relay outages are retried.
- Closed/crashed browsers are restarted with increasing delays.
- Supplier sessions are rechecked in the existing browser profile.
- Saved credentials are used only on the exact ESP origin and verified login form.
- Login retries are limited to one every 30 minutes in the running worker.

LIMITS
A changed login form, CAPTCHA, two-factor challenge, incorrect password or pricing
settings still requires attention on the PC. This is not remote desktop access.
Keep the worker window open; the PC must be awake and connected to the internet.
The installer retains the existing Edge profile and Haynes pairing and backs up
an existing installation in its managed folder. Other worker launchers are not
rewired: start tyres with the new dedicated desktop shortcut.
No orders or cart actions are performed. Live supplier testing is still required
on your workshop PC; automated recovery and website regression tests passed here.
